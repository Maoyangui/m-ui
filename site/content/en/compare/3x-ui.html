m-ui vs 3x-ui

3x-ui is the most popular x-ui panel and manages Xray-core. m-ui manages sing-box and embeds it. That single difference drives most of what follows. Facts checked on 2026-09-05 against 3x-ui's README and releases (v3.7.0, 2026-08-24); anything the README does not state is marked Unknown.

The one-paragraph version

If you want Xray — XTLS Vision and other flows, XHTTP, WireGuard and TUN inbounds, the widest client compatibility and by far the largest community — 3x-ui is the obvious choice and m-ui is not a substitute. If you want sing-box (Hysteria2, AnyTLS, TUIC, Reality via sing-box) with a panel that embeds the core, applies user changes without restarts, validates and rolls back configuration, and keeps several servers in sync from one master, m-ui is the alternative you are looking for.

Choose 3x-ui if

  • You need Xray-specific transports or inbounds (XHTTP, WireGuard, TUN, MTProto) or want to stay on the Xray ecosystem your users already know.
  • You value the largest community, the most third-party guides and years of production use.
  • You want hardware-ID device limits or MTProto — features m-ui does not have.

Choose m-ui if

  • You have decided on sing-box — for Hysteria2, AnyTLS or TUIC, or simply because your clients are sing-box based.
  • You run several servers and want one user table, one quota and one device limit across all of them, without cloning inbounds by hand.
  • You change users often and do not want the core restarted each time.
  • You want configuration validated by the core itself before it is saved, with rollback if it still fails to start.
  • You want one binary, no Docker, no separate core to version-match.

Migrating

m-ui does not claim to import 3x-ui's database: the importer targets one compatible legacy format and reports exactly what it mapped. Moving from 3x-ui means re-creating lines (a few minutes with quick-add) and either re-creating users or bulk-creating them from a plan; user credentials will change, so clients re-import the subscription once. Read Getting started first.
